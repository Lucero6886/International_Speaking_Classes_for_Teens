Buying Things Online 🛒

Module 3 · Week 31 — Digital Life, Choices, and Well-being

2. Vocabulary 📖

Words for shopping online. Tap a card for an example.

online store🛒
A website or app where you can buy things.
"Shopee is a popular online store in Vietnam."
price💰
How much money something costs.
"The price of the shoes was really good."
discount🏷️
Money off the normal price.
"I got a twenty percent discount on my new headphones."
delivery🚚
Bringing goods to your home.
"Delivery only took three days."
review⭐
A customer's comment about a product.
"I always read the reviews before I buy."
order🖱️
To ask a shop to send you something.
"I ordered a new phone case yesterday."
compare⚖️
To look at two things and see the differences.
"I always compare prices on different websites."
refund💸
Money given back when you return something.
"I got a refund because the shirt was too small."
secure payment🔒
A safe way to pay for something online.
"Always check for secure payment before you pay."
worth it👍
Good value for the money you pay.
"The jacket was expensive, but it was worth it."

4. Role-Play 🎭

Act these out with a partner. Tap Show hints if you get stuck.

🎬 Scenario 1 — Choosing between two products

You and your friend are looking at two pairs of headphones online. Compare the price, reviews and features, then decide together which one to buy.

🧑 Student A — Shopper 1🧑 Student B — Shopper 2
  • A: "Look, this pair is 300,000 dong and has four stars. The other one is 500,000 dong with five stars."
  • B: "The cheaper one has good reviews too. What do people say about the sound quality?"
  • A: "It says the sound is great, but the battery is short. The expensive one has a longer battery."
  • B: "I think the more expensive one is worth it because battery life is important for me."
  • A: "Okay, let's check the delivery time too before we decide."

🎬 Scenario 2 — A problem with your order

You ordered a jacket online, but it arrived in the wrong size. Contact the online shop and ask for a refund or an exchange.

🧑 Student A — Customer🧑 Student B — Customer service
  • A: "Hi, I ordered a jacket last week, but it's too small for me."
  • B: "I'm sorry to hear that. Would you like a refund or an exchange for a bigger size?"
  • A: "I'd like an exchange, please. Can I get it in a large?"
  • B: "Of course. Please send the jacket back, and we'll send a new one this week."
  • A: "Thank you! How long will the new delivery take?"
