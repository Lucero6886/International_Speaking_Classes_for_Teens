2. Vocabulary 📖

Words about sleep, energy and morning routines. Tap a card for an example.

routine🔁
A series of things you do regularly, in the same order.
"My morning routine takes about thirty minutes."
exhausted🥱
Extremely tired.
"I felt exhausted after staying up late."
energetic⚡
Full of energy and enthusiasm.
"She's always energetic in the morning."
snooze😪
To press a button so an alarm rings again a few minutes later.
"I always snooze my alarm at least twice."
screen time📱
The amount of time you spend using a phone, tablet or computer.
"Too much screen time before bed can keep you awake."
wind down🌙
To relax before going to sleep.
"I like to wind down by reading a book."
oversleep🛌
To sleep longer than you planned.
"I overslept and missed the school bus."
bedtime🕙
The time you normally go to sleep.
"My bedtime on school nights is ten o'clock."
habit🔄
Something you do often, almost without thinking.
"Drinking water in the morning is a good habit."
refreshed🌤️
Feeling rested and full of energy after sleep.
"I feel refreshed after eight hours of sleep."

4. Role-Play 🎭

Act these out with a partner. Tap Show hints if you get stuck.

🎬 Scenario 1 — Helping a tired friend

Your friend always feels exhausted at school. Ask about their sleep habits and suggest ways to feel more energetic.

🧑 Student A — Tired friend🧑 Student B — Gives advice
  • B: "You look exhausted today. What time did you go to bed?"
  • A: "I went to bed really late. I was on my phone."
  • B: "Maybe you should wind down without your phone before bed."
  • A: "That's a good idea. What else can I try?"
  • B: "Try going to bed at the same time every night — you'll feel more refreshed."

🎬 Scenario 2 — Talking to a parent about phone use before bed

A teen wants to use their phone before sleeping. A parent is worried it affects their sleep and energy. Find a compromise.

🧑 Student A — Teen🧑 Student B — Parent
  • B: "You always oversleep. I think it's your screen time before bed."
  • A: "I understand, but I just want to chat with my friends a little."
  • B: "What about a bedtime routine — phone off thirty minutes before bed?"
  • A: "Okay, I can try that. Can I still read on my phone for ten minutes?"
  • B: "That sounds fair. Let's try it this week and see how you feel."
